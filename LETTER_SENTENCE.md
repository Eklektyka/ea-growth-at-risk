# Letter Sentences

Two alternative sentences for a motivation letter, each under 40 words,
using only numbers from `results/paper_spec.csv`.

## Option A

I replicated the growth-at-risk framework of Figueres & Jarociński (2020)
for the euro area: the CISS coefficient at the 10th percentile of
year-ahead growth is −16.91, roughly five times the OLS estimate of −3.48.

## Option B

Using quantile regressions on 78 quarters of euro area data, I found that
higher financial stress (CISS) lowers the 10th percentile of year-ahead
GDP growth (coefficient −14.98) far more than the median (−4.76),
reproducing the paper's main finding.
